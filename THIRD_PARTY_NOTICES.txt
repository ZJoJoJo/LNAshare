LANShare uses Python 3.13, Tcl/Tk, pyftpdlib, Paramiko, psutil,
cryptography, bcrypt, PyNaCl, cffi, pycparser, invoke, pyasyncore,
pyasynchat, and PyInstaller runtime components.

Upstream license texts and dependency metadata are distributed in
third-party-licenses/. Bundled packages also retain upstream package data.
